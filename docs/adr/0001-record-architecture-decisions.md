# Record architecture decisions

We record decisions that are hard to reverse, surprising without context, and the result of a real trade-off as short ADRs in `docs/adr/`, numbered in order. Each states the decision, why, and what it forces on later work. Terms follow `GLOSSARY.md`.

## Consequences

- A change that contradicts an ADR says so in its PR and either supersedes the ADR or reopens it; it does not silently override it.
- Small, easily reversed choices do not get an ADR.
